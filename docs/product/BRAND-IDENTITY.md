# Brand Identity

## Strategic direction
Blend two qualities: **precision of a premium camera instrument** and **imagination of a futuristic creative technology studio**. Creative technology leads the emotional tone; photographic precision earns trust.

## Working name
LumaLab is the repository/product working name. Treat it as provisional until trademark, domain, app-store and pronunciation checks are complete. Do not imply legal availability.

## Positioning statement
A virtual photography studio where people explore light, optics and image-making by doing—not just reading.

## Brand promise
Understand light. Master the image.

## Personality
- Precise, not clinical.
- Cinematic, not theatrical.
- Intelligent, not intimidating.
- Experimental, not gimmicky.
- Premium, not exclusive.

## Visual principles
1. The photograph/3D scene is the hero; UI chrome stays quiet.
2. Use deep neutral surfaces to make light, glass and image previews stand out.
3. Electric cool blue signals controls, focus and technical selection.
4. Warm amber is reserved for light temperature, exposure warmth and creative emphasis.
5. Fine measurement marks and optical diagrams add instrument credibility.
6. Avoid generic AI gradients, excessive glassmorphism, neon overload and decorative camera clichés.

## Typography
Use a neutral modern sans (Inter or a metrically compatible alternative) for UI. Use tabular numerals for all camera readouts. A restrained display face may be used for marketing headlines only; never sacrifice instrument readability.

## Voice
Short, clear, confident, curious. Explain technical terms in context. Prefer “Move focus to 2.5 m” over vague copy such as “Make it pop”. Never overstate optical accuracy.

## Logo direction
Develop a compact optical-aperture / focus-plane mark that remains legible at 16–24 px. Avoid literal camera silhouettes and overly complex aperture blades. Wordmark should work in monochrome and on dark/light backgrounds.

## Tagline
Primary: “Understand light. Master the image.”
Supporting: “A virtual studio for the science and art of photography.”

## Brand gates before public launch
- Complete trademark/domain/name collision review in intended markets.
- Test pronunciation and recall with beginner and professional audiences.
- Produce SVG master, app icon, monochrome, reversed and clear-space rules.
- Validate contrast and small-size legibility.
